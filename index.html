100 DAYS TO TRANSFORM YOUR HABITS.

Track the effort you control: showing up, learning good technique, studying with focus, practising skills, eating regularly, and recovering. Bodies and results vary—your goal is steady, healthy progress, not perfection.

THE ROADMAP

Four phases. One day at a time.

Move at your own pace. If you miss a day, simply restart the next day.

DAYS 1–25

Build the base

Set a realistic routine, learn exercise form, and organise your study space.

DAYS 26–50

Build consistency

Repeat the basics, log sessions, and practise skills in small steps.

DAYS 51–75

Improve your process

Review what works, refine technique, and make gradual adjustments.

DAYS 76–100

Reflect & continue

Compare habits and skills with Day 1, celebrate effort, and plan the next cycle.
